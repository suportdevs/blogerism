## Posting tips

- **LinkedIn:** upload the MP4 natively (don't paste a YouTube link) and pick `Blogerism-promo-cover.jpg` as the thumbnail. Put links in the first comment, not in the post.
- **X:** the 4:5 video plays fine in the timeline. Pin the post to your profile.
- **Facebook:** upload as a regular video post (not a Reel, which needs 9:16). The 4:5 frame fills more of the mobile feed than 16:9.
- Most feed videos autoplay muted, which is why every point is also written on screen.
